About

Hi, I’m Priscilla! I’m a senior PM who loves bringing products from 0 to 1.

At R-Zero, I ship products that cut energy use 20% for enterprise customers. Before that, I built mobile and Apple Watch apps for connected women’s health products at Willow.

I’m a mechanical engineer by training, and I build apps on the side. My latest, Postcard Press, was featured in Business Insider and has mailed 150+ postcards.

Outside of work, you’ll find me 3D printing home decor, running through Golden Gate Park, and junk journaling.
