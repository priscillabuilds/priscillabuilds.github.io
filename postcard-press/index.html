Postcard Press.

Send a postcard as easily as a text.

Try it ↗★ Featured in Business Insider
01
The problem

I love sending and receiving physical mail, but I almost never send it. You have to buy a card, write it, get a stamp, and find a blue mailbox, and with that much friction I usually just don't do it.

So I built the friction away: an app that lets you send a postcard as easily as a text.

02
Who it's for

Postcard Press keeps long-distance friends and relationships connected. It's for anyone who wants to send something real to someone far away, without hunting for stamps or a mailbox.

  • TravelersSend memories from the trip back home, right from your phone.
  • Long-distance friends & relationshipsStay in each other's lives with something you can hold.
03
Competitors

Card apps already handle printing and mailing, but they're built around designing a card, not quickly sending a thought.

  • TouchnotePolished, but subscription-first with lots of steps.
  • PostableBuilt for holidays and bulk sends, not everyday notes.
  • HallmarkTemplate-driven, so it doesn't feel like you.
04
Validation

First, I built a prototype with Claude Code, hooked up to a print-and-mail API. Before making it production-ready, I wanted to know if it was worth building. Here's what I did:

  • Demoed it in personShowed the prototype to lots of people at ProductCon, a product manager conference.
  • Set up a waitlistBuilt with Tally; it collected about 100 emails.
  • Shared the build onlineOrganic video posts reached 100K+ people.
ProductConIn-person demos

05
My solution

You upload a photo, write a note, and add an address, and Postcard Press prints and mails a real postcard. I built the first version in one night with Claude Code and the PostGrid API, then added Stripe so people could pay.
